Privacy Policy – Leonida Files Studio

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Leonida Files Studio ("the app") is a personal tool used by a single creator to prepare short videos about GTA VI news and to send them to the creator's own TikTok account, "Leonida Files". It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the app accesses

  • When the creator authorizes the app with TikTok Login Kit, the app receives an access token for that single TikTok account and basic profile information (display name, avatar).
  • With the Content Posting API, the app uploads videos that the creator has reviewed and approved, together with the caption the creator approved.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, store or process personal data of any other TikTok user.
  • It does not sell, rent or share data with third parties, and it does not use data for advertising.
  • It does not read private messages, followers or contacts.

Storage and security

The access token is stored only on the creator's own computer or private server, in a protected configuration file, and is never published. It can be revoked at any time from TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions), and deleted from the app on request.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as needed to publish the creator's videos. Revoking the app's access in TikTok ends all access immediately.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: leonidafiles1213@protonmail.com
